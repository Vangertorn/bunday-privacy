Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Notes application (the "Application") handles Your information and explains Your privacy rights.

In short: the Application does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything You enter is stored only on Your Device. The Application has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not request access to the Internet.

Interpretation and Definitions

Interpretation

Words with capitalized initial letters have the meanings defined below. The definitions apply in both singular and plural.

Definitions

  • Application refers to Notes, the mobile application for Android.
  • Developer (referred to as "We", "Us" or "Our") refers to Konstantsin Yankin, the individual developer of the Application, based in Poland.
  • Device means the phone or tablet on which You install and use the Application.
  • Personal Data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • You means the individual using the Application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any Personal Data. The Application does not have Internet access, does not create user accounts, and does not contain advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking technologies. It does not use cookies.

Data You enter in the Application

To provide its features, the Application stores the information You enter locally on Your Device, including:

  • tasks: titles, descriptions, icons, colors, dates, repeats, times, durations and reminders;
  • subtasks and the days on which You mark tasks and subtasks as done;
  • task groups and app settings;
  • notes: text, lists and colors;
  • photos and files You attach to tasks and notes.

This information never leaves Your Device unless You choose to export or share it as described below. We have no access to it.

Photos and files

You can attach a photo to a task or note by choosing an image with the system photo picker or by taking a picture with Your Device's camera app, and attach a file by choosing it in the system file picker. The Application only receives the items You pick or take and stores copies on Your Device. It does not request access to Your whole photo library, to Your files or to the camera.

Notifications and widget

If You allow notifications, the Application shows local reminders about Your tasks. Reminders are created on Your Device; no notification data is sent anywhere. Reminders and the home screen widget show task titles, so anyone who can see Your screen may see them.

How Your Data Can Leave Your Device

Data leaves Your Device only through actions that You initiate or settings You control:

  • Backups You export. You can save a backup file or enable automatic backups to a folder You select. The file is written only to the location You choose. If that location is synced with a cloud service, that service's privacy policy applies.
  • Sharing. You can share a backup file, or share a task as text with a link through the Android share menu. The content goes only to the app or person You select. The task details are contained in the link itself, after the "#" sign, so anyone who has the link can read them. If the link is opened in a browser, the page vangertorn.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages, shows the task on that person's device; browsers do not send the part after "#" to the server.
  • Android system backup. If Android backup is enabled on Your Device, Android may include the Application's data in Your device backup stored in Your Google Account, and transfer it to a new device. This backup is performed by Google, not by Us, under the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn it off in Your Device settings. Starting with Android 9, photos and files You attach are not included in the backup stored in Your Google Account; they are only copied during a direct transfer to a new device.
  • Importing. When You open a backup file or a task link, the Application reads only that file or link.

Use of Your Data

The data stored on Your Device is used only to provide the Application's features: keeping Your tasks and notes, showing the calendar, reminders and widget, and creating backups. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Sharing of Your Data

We do not share, sell or transfer Your data to third parties, because We never receive it.

Retention and Deletion of Your Data

Your data stays on Your Device for as long as You keep it there. You can delete it at any time:

  • by deleting tasks and notes in the Application: they move to the trash and are permanently deleted after 7 days, or when You delete them from the trash;
  • by clearing the Application's data in Your Device settings;
  • by uninstalling the Application.

Backup files You exported and backups made by Android are kept where they were saved until You delete them.

Security of Your Data

Your data is stored in the Application's private storage on Your Device, which other apps cannot access. Please keep backup files You export in a safe place, because they contain Your tasks, notes and attachments in readable form.

Your Rights

Because We do not collect or hold any Personal Data about You, We have no data to provide, correct or delete on request. You have full control over Your data in the Application and can view, change, export or delete it at any time. If You have any questions, contact Us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from anyone, including children.

Links to Other Websites

The Application contains a link to the Developer's LinkedIn page and an option to write to Us by email. These open in other apps that are not operated by Us. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party sites or services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page and the "Last updated" date at the top is updated. If the Application starts collecting or sharing data, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect.
